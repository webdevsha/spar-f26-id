How AI deployment and Indonesia's adjustment buffer could reshape bank employment by 2030

Ten signals, twenty-five verified indicators, sixteen policy instruments — and a measurement system that would not show the shock arriving. This monitor sets out what Indonesia can see, what it cannot, and what each of the four possible worlds would require of the six bodies that can act.

Key findings

Four things stand out — and three of them are about what cannot be seen

  1. No instrument in Indonesia requires anyone to report an AI adoption rate.invisible OJK holds financial and technology-risk reporting; Kemnaker holds vacancy data. Neither publishes adoption. The best number available is a voluntary survey of 91 banks: 34% piloting, 18% operational, 4% across functions (Oct 2026).
  2. No AI-linked outlook exists anywhere in the system.invisible No ministry, regulator or industry body publishes a jobs or revenue forecast that names AI — so every such forecast in Indonesia today is voluntary or foreign.
  3. The claims series carries two legal breaks inside twenty months.broken series PP 6/2025 moved the scheme on 7 Feb 2025; UU 27/2026 landed 28 Oct 2026 with no transition period and implementing rules not due until 28 Oct 2027. Any 2025-to-2026 comparison carries that break.
  4. Nothing follows a worker after they are dismissed.trail stops JKK and JKM pay out whether a person resigned or retired; Kartu Prakerja records who joined training, not where they ended up. Displacement can be counted at the point of dismissal, not traced.

The four worlds

Two axes we can evidence; one quadrant we deliberately do not draw

pace of AI deployment in banking →

Managed Adjustment

Slow deployment · buffer holds

Hiring thins rather than collapses. Exits and turnover absorb the change; JKP claims rise gently and stay inside the scheme's own standard.

Read scenario →

Quiet Squeeze

Slow deployment · buffer thin

Entry-level hiring falls first and quietly. Nothing catches it: vacancies are counted, filling is not; no destination is tracked. The pressure shows up late, in other sectors.

Read scenario →

Absorbed Shock

Fast deployment · buffer holds

Displacement is real and visible, and JKP, Prakerja and PEKA are visibly load-bearing. The question becomes adequacy — 60% of wage for six months, paid within three working days.

Read scenario →

Few Winners, Many Leavers

Fast deployment · buffer thin

Whole clerical functions go. 23,538 bank offices (Jun 2025, −2.6% in a year) and 53,490 layoffs recorded Jan–Aug 2026 are the leading edge — with the series breaking exactly when they matter most.

Not covered: fast deployment with an untested buffer

Not drawn as a fifth world, because our buffer evidence cannot separate “the buffer worked” from “we stopped looking”. JKP's own timing is never measured; no destination is tracked. Drawing it would imply a resolution our data does not have.
