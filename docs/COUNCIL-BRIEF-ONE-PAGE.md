# Indonesia's next bank job shock will not announce itself

**One page for AI councils · 28 November 2026 · SPAR Indonesia**

**The finding.** Indonesian banks are still hiring on net — eight large banks added 8,490 more people than
they lost last year. Banking is nonetheless the most AI-exposed sector measured in the country: a mean
exposure of **0.582** across 20,088 banking advertisements, the highest of 29 job categories. The risk is not
that AI dismisses bank staff. It is that banks stop hiring them, quietly, and no instrument notices.

**Why nothing notices.** Every worker-protection instrument we inventoried starts when someone is
**dismissed**. JKP pays 60% of wage up to Rp5m for six months, and in 2025 it paid 90,513 workers Rp1.01 tn —
but it triggers on layoff. In our slow and medium cases **nobody is dismissed**: the same frozen headcount is
reached by not replacing leavers. That shock is invisible to JKP by design, and the national claims series
cannot see it either.

**What the numbers say now.** Eight banks, 218,129 staff. By 2030 the panel is 1.0% smaller in the slow case,
6.9% in the medium and 23.3% in the extreme. Dismissals are zero, zero and 25,826 — because attrition absorbs
the first two entirely.

**Four asks**

| Who | What | By when |
|---|---|---|
OJK | Add a workforce-transition note — hires, leavers, roles changed, retraining — to the Q2 2027 supervisory dialogue on AI use, starting with these eight banks | Q2 2027 |
Kemnaker with BPS | Publish bank-function vacancy **and fill-status** series from Pasker ID, plus a finance (KBLI K) by-occupation cut of Sakernas | Feb 2027 |
BPJS Ketenagakerjaan and Kemnaker | Publish the reasons behind the 17% JKP rejections and the finance share of claims; pre-position Voc50k and Prakerja places for clerical and frontline roles | by 28 Oct 2027 |
Komdigi, Setneg, AI councils | Add a workforce-impact line to AI Perpres drafts before signature, and name an owner for a hiring-side trigger | before signature |

**What we cannot yet say.** Whether any of this is happening: no Indonesian series links a bank's AI use to its
staffing, no source follows a worker after they leave, and no agency commits to watching an AI-uptake signal.
Everything above is a stress test against that absence, not a forecast.
