# Decision note: exposed share x is the role mix (28 Nov 2026, v1.0)

Supersedes the choice in DECISIONS-28NOV.md that adopted the postings index 0.582 as x.

**Decision.** x (the share of bank staff in roles AI can touch) is set from the role mix of 144 verified banking postings: 25.7% core exposed roles, 65.3% including credit and loan roles, midpoint 0.45. The postings index 0.582 is kept as a cross-check on task exposure e, not as x.

**Why.** The index is already a task-exposure measure, so using it as x and then multiplying by e counts exposure twice. The index sits closest to e Medium (0.53), which supports Medium as the central case.

**Effect (panel of 8 banks, 2027 to 2030).**

| | Slow | Medium | Extreme |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hires not made, x = 0.45 | 1,917 | 13,001 | 29,257 |
| Dismissals, x = 0.45 | 0 | 0 | 15,702 |
| Hires not made, x = 0.582 | 2,480 | 16,814 | n/a |
| Dismissals, x = 0.582 | 0 | 0 | 27,578 |

Medium has zero dismissals at every x up to 0.65. Extreme dismissals are 0 at x = 0.26 and 33.7k at x = 0.65, so Extreme is sensitive to x.

**Still open.** x remains a placeholder until GRI 2-7 or Sakernas KBJI by KBLI K gives the real role mix. Needs a team call.
