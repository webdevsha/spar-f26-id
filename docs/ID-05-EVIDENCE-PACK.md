**"Digital Sovereign AI and the Future of Trusted Banking"** — PERBANAS × IBM × K&A, **34 pages**, launched

## 4 · The thing that actually moves the study: a speed series

ID-05's fatal weakness was one observation. OJK's banking supervisor (Dian Ediana Rae), reported by CNBC
Indonesia and corroborated by Kontan and Bisnis, gives **three points** for the share of banks adopting AI:

> **≈30% (2024) → 42% (2025) → 57.9% (Q1 2026)**

That is the adoption *speed* the scenarios were assuming. Two cautions, both recorded in the row:

1. **It is a different measure** from the 34 / 18 / 4 stage split — share of banks *adopting at all*, not
   the stage of the respondents surveyed. The two must never be added or interchanged.
2. **Confidence M, not H** — it is a regulator's statement reported by press, not a published table. The
   underlying series is not public.
