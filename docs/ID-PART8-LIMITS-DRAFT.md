## Limits

**The middle of the chain is missing.** No Indonesian series links workplace AI adoption to hires,
separations or wages. We can observe capability (named systems in bank disclosures) and we can
observe strain (JKP benefit payments, 431,086 to 90,513 workers in 2025), but the hiring link
between them is where the country's monitoring is thinnest. Every finding in this lane should be
read with that gap in view.

**Four further limits, each on the record:**

- **No firm-level adoption data.** Indonesia has no representative firm-level generative-AI adoption
  series. The diffusion figures used as context are vendor telemetry and are graded low-confidence.
- **A posting sample is a sample.** For 2024 our finance-scoped rows number 66 against Kemnaker's own
  726 — about 9%. Any share we quote is a share of a sample, and the sample size must travel with it.
- **Ad text carries little signal.** Only 3 of 2,618 collected ads name an AI skill or tool, so the
  posts themselves cannot evidence adoption.
- **Archive volume is not hiring.** Archived and crawled counts vary with the crawl, so archive
  series are compared only within one platform, only for captures within 90 days of posting, and
  only across calendar months both years share. Counts, job cards and full pages are three different
  questions and are never added together.

**Two structural caveats.** PP 6/2025 changed JKP generosity, claim timing and contributions from
7 February 2025, so 2024 and 2025 claims are not directly comparable — higher claims may reflect
access rather than job loss. And no series follows dismissed formal workers into informal work
(87.74m informal against 59.93m formal, Feb 2026), so exit from formal payroll becomes invisible.

## Next steps

Close the readiness scoring once the six question texts are available; secure the BPS/Kemnaker
interview on data gaps (question drafted); request the Karirhub detailed extract; and strengthen the
