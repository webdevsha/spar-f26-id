## The evidence each question can draw on (already collected — no new research needed)

**A. Coverage and reach**
- JKP: 431,086 benefit payments to 90,513 workers in 2025; 237,550 claim cases (2023), 250,594 (2024). A 3-working-day payment standard is published; the *achieved* median is not.
- Pasker ID: finance & insurance postings 1,195 → 797 → 726 (2022–24); portal total 10,695 → 20,027 → 17,169; occupation table by KBJI (clerical: 1,751 → 3,341 → 2,034).
- OJK AI guide: covers banks' AI governance; no published reach or take-up measure located.

**B. Who is reached, and who is missed**
- JKP reach is limited by formality: national informal workers 87.74m against 59.93m formal (Feb 2026). Payroll-based schemes cannot reach informal work.
- 17% of JKP applications were reported rejected for documentation problems (May 2025 hearing, confidence **M** — one hearing, year-to-date).
- Pasker ID reaches advertised vacancies only; whether a posting is filled, and by whom, is not recorded.

**C. Trigger and timing**
- JKP triggers on eligible layoff, but **PP 6/2025 changed generosity, claim timing and contribution conditions from 7 Feb 2025** — so 2024/2025 levels are not comparable, and a rising claim count may be access, not job loss.
- The published JKP service standard is 3 working days; achieved performance is unpublished.
- No published trigger ties any instrument to an AI-uptake threshold.

**D. Funding**
- JKP is contribution-funded (0.36% of monthly wage under PP 6/2025); the financing line and its adequacy under a surge are **not published** in the sources reviewed.
- Pasker ID is a portal, not a benefit; cost basis not applicable. OJK guidance is supervisory, not funded support.

**E. Delivery capacity (does help arrive in time?)**
- Achieved JKP approval → first-payment latency: **not published**.
- Training follow-through: ILO review (2022) reports 21 reskilled and 39 placed, reported separately, not linked; PEKA reports 1,426 recipients across 45 locations (Aug 2026). No placement or earnings outcome series for ex-bank workers.
- Bank-level skills response exists: BCA reports 2,550 employees in AI-related training (2025).

**F. Monitoring and revision**
- Nothing in any of the three documents commits a named agency to watch an AI-uptake signal, or to revise the instrument when one moves.
- Indonesia has **no representative firm-level AI adoption series** and **no regulator/industry forecast revision that names AI** — the two blind spots on the register.
