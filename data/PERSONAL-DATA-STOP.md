# PERSONAL-DATA-STOP

Nothing in this repository may contain personal data.

**Hard rule.** The synced corpus, the inventory, the readiness grid and every
future `data/*.csv` must be **public-sources only** — laws, regulations,
circulars, strategies, roadmaps, programme pages, public budgets, published
statistics.

**Never commit:** worker-survey responses, interview transcripts, participant
names, contact details, employer-identifying data, or anything supplied to the
project under a confidentiality understanding. The worker-survey pipeline is
separately consent-gated (Kairos approval) and is out of scope here.

**Consent gate.** Anything shown outside the team needs AISA consent first.
Default is private. The Pages URL sits behind an access gate until that consent
exists.

If you are unsure whether a file is safe to commit, stop and ask — do not
commit it "just for now".
